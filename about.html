Our Story

We Believe the Unexplained Deserves Serious Investigation

Vaultedd was born from a simple frustration: the world's most compelling mysteries were buried under clickbait, sensationalism, and poor research. We set out to change that.

Our Mission

Vaultedd is an independent mystery and paranormal research publication dedicated to exploring the world's most enduring unexplained phenomena. We cover cryptids, UFO encounters, ancient mysteries, paranormal events, and mythological creatures — all through a lens of serious research and journalistic integrity.

We believe that curiosity is one of the most important human traits. Whether Bigfoot turns out to be a genuine undiscovered primate or the product of cultural mythology, the investigation itself — the eyewitness accounts, the physical evidence, the cultural history — is worth taking seriously. We treat every mystery with the respect it deserves.

Every article on Vaultedd is deeply researched, drawing on historical records, witness testimonies, scientific studies, government documents, and expert opinions. We clearly distinguish between what is documented fact and what remains speculation. We do not manufacture drama — the real stories are compelling enough.

Content Disclaimer

Articles on Vaultedd explore mysteries, paranormal phenomena, cryptids, UFO sightings, and unexplained events. Content is based on documented historical accounts, eyewitness reports, folklore records, and published research. While we strive for accuracy, many topics covered on this site remain subjects of genuine scientific and historical debate. Articles are written for educational and entertainment purposes. Readers are encouraged to think critically and conduct their own research. Vaultedd does not claim that any specific paranormal phenomenon has been definitively proven or disproven.
